# Exhibit E — Independent Inspection Report (Kovač Inspection Services)

**Date of inspection:** 2025-07-20  
**Summary findings (non-exhaustive):**
1) Weather sealing around the control box cable entry appears incomplete.
2) Cable routing shows insufficient strain relief at two points.
3) Fuse rating observed does not match the pump’s documented start current (manual not provided to inspector).
4) Evidence of moisture ingress is consistent with reported flooding.

**Recommendations:**
- Replace damaged controller components.
- Re-route cables and add strain relief.
- Confirm fuse sizing based on pump model and controller specifications.
