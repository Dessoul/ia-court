# Exhibit A — Infrastructure Installation Contract (Extract)

**Date:** 2025-04-12  
**Parties:** RiverGate Settlements LLC (“Client”) and Danube Works LLC (“Contractor”)

## Scope
- Design and install: (i) solar PV array; (ii) battery storage; (iii) inverter; (iv) water pump and basic controls.
- Provide basic operating instructions.

## Price & payment
- Total price: LLD 135,000
- Milestones:
  1) 30% on signature
  2) 40% on delivery of hardware to site
  3) 30% on substantial completion (defined as the system powering the pump for 8 hours of normal operation)

## Target date
- Target completion: 2025-06-30

## Warranty / defects
- Contractor to remedy defects notified within 30 days of completion.

## Liability (simplified)
- Each party shall act with reasonable care to prevent damage.
- Any limitation of liability shall not apply to gross negligence or willful misconduct.
