# Claimant Initial Claim — Sava Logistics LLC v Marek Vacek

## 1. Relief requested
Sava requests the Court to:
1) Order Marek to repay **LLD 31,640** (unauthorized payments) and **LLD 9,500** (associated losses).
2) Declare Marek breached his duties as director by authorizing related-party transactions without proper approval.
3) Award court costs to Sava.

## 2. Core facts
- Marek signed an "advisory" contract with a related party (Exhibit C) without a documented approval process.
- Payments were made without clear deliverables (Exhibits D, G).
- Travel expenses appear personal (Exhibit E).
- After removal, Sava demanded repayment (Exhibit I). Marek refused (Exhibit J).

## 3. Evidence list
Exhibits A–J as indexed in `evidence/`.

## 4. Legal basis (high-level)
Sava relies on:
- Company governance principles regarding director duties, authority, and conflict-of-interest management.
- Delict / civil liability principles for wrongful loss caused by negligent or disloyal conduct.
- Judicial Process rules for admissibility and procedure.
