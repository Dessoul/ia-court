# Claimant Initial Claim — Danube Works LLC v RiverGate Settlements LLC

## 1. Parties
Claimant: Danube Works LLC (“DW”).  
Defendant: RiverGate Settlements LLC (“RG”).

## 2. Relief requested
DW requests the Court to:
1) Order RG to pay **LLD 48,500** plus reasonable interest from 2025-08-01.  
2) Award court costs to DW.

## 3. Facts
3.1 Contract. On 2025-04-12 DW and RG signed a contract for a solar micro-grid and water pump installation (Exhibit A).  
3.2 Change request. RG requested upgrades (Exhibit I). DW implemented the changes and sourced additional components.  
3.3 Delivery and completion. DW installed the system and provided basic operational guidance. DW notified RG that the system was substantially complete on 2025-07-06 (Exhibit D).  
3.4 Invoice. DW issued the final invoice LLD 48,500 (Exhibit C).  
3.5 Non-payment. RG has not paid, alleging defects. DW disputes that the system was materially defective and states that RG interfered with operation and made unauthorized adjustments.

## 4. Evidence list
Exhibits A–D, I, J as indexed in `evidence/`.

## 5. Legal basis (high-level)
DW relies on:
- Liberland Court’s jurisdiction for disputes between parties within Liberland’s jurisdiction, and procedural rules of the Judicial Process framework.
- General civil principles for enforcing payment obligations arising from agreements, and the absence of proof that alleged defects excuse total non-payment.
