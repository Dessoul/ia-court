# Claimant Reply to Counterclaim — Danube Works LLC

1) DW denies negligence. The installation followed the component manuals provided by the manufacturers.  
2) DW alleges the flooding incident resulted from **operator misuse** (running pump dry, bypassing safety settings) and from RG’s storage of chemical containers near electrical equipment (see Exhibit D email notes).  
3) DW disputes the amount of claimed damages, arguing RG’s third-party repair costs are inflated and include upgrades unrelated to any alleged defect.

DW maintains its request for payment of the invoice, with any set-off limited (if at all) to proven, proportionate losses.
