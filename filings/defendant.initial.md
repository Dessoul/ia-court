# Defendant Response & Counterclaim — RiverGate Settlements LLC

## A. Response to claim
1) RG admits a contract exists (Exhibit A) and that DW issued an invoice (Exhibit C).  
2) RG denies that the invoice is payable in full because DW delivered **unsafe and defective work** and failed to meet the promised completion date.  
3) RG denies any “interference” beyond normal operation and denies any unauthorized modifications before defects were reported.

## B. Counterclaim (Delict)
### 1. Relief requested
RG requests the Court to:
1) Dismiss or reduce DW’s claim.  
2) Order DW to pay **LLD 72,300** in damages (repairs, replacement parts, inspection costs, downtime).  
3) Award RG its court costs.

### 2. Facts supporting counterclaim
2.1 The control box was not properly weather-sealed; wiring was routed without adequate strain relief; and fuse sizing did not match the pump start current (see independent inspection, Exhibit E).  
2.2 On 2025-07-14, a pump restart triggered a controller fault and resulted in water leakage into the equipment shed, damaging batteries and inverter components (Exhibits E & F).  
2.3 RG notified DW promptly and requested repair. DW did not attend within a reasonable time (Exhibit D).  
2.4 RG engaged third-party repairs to prevent further losses (Exhibit J).

### 3. Causation / mitigation
RG contends DW’s negligent installation was the primary cause. RG acted to mitigate by securing an inspection and urgent repairs.
